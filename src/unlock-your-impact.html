My story

I've been in your shoes.

I spent 5 years trying to get promoted. I knew I had a ton of potential and the drive to keep advancing, yet I felt taken for granted the whole time.

I remember admiring the peers around me who always seemed to get the recognition and praise. They talked about their work regularly.

Good work is ultimately invisible work if no one sees it. Over time, I realized that getting it seen is a skill that can be learned, and one we need if we want to advance.

There are ways to talk about your work that feel authentic instead of braggy or slimy, and they still help you reach your goals.

I'm excited to show you how to do just that!

Why this matters now

Being good at your job isn't the protection it used to be.

Most high performers were never taught how to make that happen. They were told to do great work and trust that the right people would notice.
